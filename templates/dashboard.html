    SUPPORT
    Frequently Asked Questions
    Everything you need to know before your first live session.
    Which brokers does GRID·ML support? ▾
    GRID·ML connects via the official MetaTrader 5 Python API and works with any broker that runs MT5 — including IC Markets, Exness, Pepperstone, XM, Roboforex, and FTMO demo accounts. All you need is your MT5 login, password, and server name from your broker's welcome email.
    Is it safe to run on a live funded account? ▾
    We strongly recommend running a demo account for at least two weeks before going live. That said, the built-in risk controls — daily DD cap, per-trade risk%, hard SL, and emergency stop — are specifically designed to protect funded accounts. Start with minimal lot sizes (0.01) and scale up as you gain confidence in the model's performance on your broker.
    How does the Random Forest model get trained? ▾
    On first connect, the bot fetches 50,000+ historical M1 bars from your broker and trains a Random Forest classifier on 9 features: EMA9, EMA21, RSI, MACD signal, ATR bands, candle body ratio, close momentum, volume delta, and hour-of-day. Training takes ~15–90 seconds depending on your hardware. You can retrain at any time via the ↺ RETRAIN button.
    What does the daily drawdown cap actually do? ▾
    Once your account equity drops by more than the configured Max Daily DD% (default 2.0%) from the session opening balance, the bot automatically stops opening new grid levels and sets all pending orders to close-only mode. It does not force-close existing positions, but the DD meter in the Risk Manager card turns red and the bot stays paused until midnight reset.
    Can I trade multiple symbols at once? ▾
    Currently each running instance handles one symbol. To trade multiple pairs simultaneously, run separate server instances on different ports and point each to the same MT5 terminal. Multi-symbol support with shared drawdown tracking is on the roadmap for a future release.
    What server/hardware do I need? ▾
    The bot runs fine on a Windows VPS with 2 vCPU and 4 GB RAM. MetaTrader 5 terminal must be installed on the same machine as the server. A low-latency connection to your broker (ping <20ms) is recommended for scalping. Linux is not currently supported because MT5 terminal is Windows-only.
    Does the bot trade during high-impact news? ▾
    Not automatically — the current version does not include a built-in economic calendar filter. We recommend manually pausing the bot (PAUSE button in the topbar) 5 minutes before scheduled high-impact releases such as NFP, CPI, FOMC, and central bank rate decisions. A calendar-aware pause filter is planned for v2.
    Is my MT5 password stored anywhere? ▾
    Your credentials are passed directly to the MetaTrader 5 Python API running locally on your own machine. They are never transmitted to or stored on any external server. The dashboard WebSocket connection is also local (localhost). We never have access to your broker account.
